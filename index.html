01 / Platform

One layer between your team and the busywork.

AI agents, workflows, approvals and operational signals in one command center.

✦

AI agents

Focused agents for research, support, operations and analysis — each with a defined role and execution boundary.

02 / Agent layer

Give every workflow a specialist.

OrbitOps treats AI as an operational layer, not a chatbot bolted onto your product. Assign a role, define the tools, set approval rules, and let the agent execute.
